# Privacy Policy for IWP

**Last Updated:** June 30, 2026

## 1. Introduction

IWP ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") is a task-based lifestyle management application designed for women, helping users organize and manage daily tasks and personal goals through AI-assisted planning. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use IWP.

By using IWP, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

To provide our task management and AI planning features, we collect:

- **Account Information:** Such as your name, email address, and authentication credentials, used to create and secure your account.
- **Task and Planning Data:** Tasks, goals, schedules, and related entries you create within the App.
- **AI Plan Context:** Information you provide to generate personalized plans (e.g., preferences, life-area details, and task history), which is used to improve the relevance of AI-generated suggestions.
- **Usage Data:** General information about how you interact with the App, such as feature usage and session activity, used to maintain and improve the service.
- **Payment Information:** Handled entirely through Google Play and Apple App Store billing systems (see Section 6). We do not collect or store your payment card details.

We do **not** require access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, photos, or any other device-level permissions to use the App's core features.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide, operate, and maintain the App's task and planning features
- Generate personalized AI-driven plans and recommendations
- Sync your data securely across your devices, so it is available anytime, anywhere
- Improve the App's functionality, performance, and user experience
- Communicate with you regarding updates, support, or account-related matters
- Process subscription and purchase transactions

## 4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on our own servers to ensure it remains safe and accessible to you across any device, at any time. We apply industry-standard security measures, including:

- Encrypted data transmission between your device and our servers
- Access-controlled cloud storage
- Authenticated, user-scoped data access, meaning your data is only accessible through your own authenticated account

While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 5. App Permissions

IWP is designed to function with **no required device permissions**. The App does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage, or other sensitive device features to deliver its core task-management and planning functionality.

## 6. Payments and Subscriptions

All purchases and subscriptions within IWP are processed through:

- **Google Play Billing** (for Android users)
- **Apple App Store / Apple Pay** (for iOS users)

These transactions are handled entirely by Google and Apple in accordance with their respective payment and privacy policies. We do not receive, process, or store your credit card or payment account details. Please refer to Google Play's and Apple's privacy policies for information on how they handle your payment data.

## 7. Third-Party Services

IWP relies on the following third-party services to operate:

- **Firebase Cloud Messaging:** Used solely to deliver push notifications (such as reminders and updates) to your device. Your task and account data is not stored on Firebase.
- **AI Plan Generation Service:** To generate personalized plans, relevant context from your tasks and preferences is processed through a third-party AI service. This data is used solely to generate your personalized plan and is not used to identify you to third parties.
- **App Store Platforms:** Google Play and Apple App Store for distribution and payment processing.

These providers may process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy obligations.

## 8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your data with third parties for marketing purposes. Data is only shared with the service providers described in Section 7, strictly to the extent necessary to operate the App.

## 9. Data Retention

We retain your account and task data for as long as your account remains active, so that your information stays available across all your devices. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see Section 11).

## 10. Children's Privacy

IWP is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required by your local laws). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

## 11. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your account and associated data
- Withdraw consent to data processing, where applicable

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 13.
